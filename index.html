BEHIND THE NAME

Komron Rafiew — the Tajik power out of Salzburg, training at Cole's Gym and Thai Boxing MMA SBG. An experienced amateur MMA fighter with over 15 fights and the current Bantamweight Champion.

His greatest strength: dominant grappling paired with explosive athleticism. Trained at Cole's Gym & Thai Boxing MMA SBG — both known for their sharp Thaiboxing — he brings an aggressive mix of pressure, power and technique to the Bantamweight division.

At BRAVE VI in Vienna he proved it — winning the title and cementing his place at the top of the Austrian amateur scene.
